This is a short English wiki for **Sloppy Forgeries**, Steam AppID **3030560**, built as a static site for **sloppyforgeries.wiki**.

## Disclaimer

sloppyforgeries.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for *Sloppy Forgeries*. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Playful Systems LLC, ENJOYN GAMES, Valve, or Steam**. All game content, names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. This site does not sell the game, process purchases, or recover accounts.

Nothing here is account support. The Steam store page lists https://playfulsystems.com/ and jonah@playfulsystems.com. The official site printed on that page is https://sloppyforgeries.com/. That site and that mailbox belong to the developer, not to this wiki. This wiki does not read jonah@playfulsystems.com.

The [guide](/guide), [painting](/painting), [accuracy](/accuracy), and [multiplayer](/multiplayer) pages quote dated Steam text. A painting title appears when the store pitch or a store image used it, and the painting page says that a screenshot is not the catalog. There is no codes page. The join code is a session code for online play. The sources checked do not publish a redeem program.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 12 Nov 2026” means the Steam API, the English store page, the demo listing, and the official site were read that day. It does not mean someone finished the launch build. The full game was still marked coming soon, with a planned date of 13 Nov 2026.
</Callout>

## Images

The library hero, the library header, capsules, the store header, and screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 3030560. They were downloaded from the Steam CDN and are served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. The file list and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

## Privacy

The site is static. It has no accounts and does not ask for your Steam login.

`analytics.gaId` is an empty string. The base layout does not load Google Analytics.

The Adsterra slot named `afterQuickAnswer` is turned off. Its id and script URL are empty. No advertising script from another game is included. Adult and sensitive categories stay off. The 404 page does not render a slot.

The same privacy note is in the footer of every page, under the heading Privacy. The disclaimer paragraph in that footer is the short form of this page.

## What is still unpublished

- A numeric Steam price. The 12 November appdetails payload had an empty package list, and `is_free` was false.
- A scoring formula, a grade chart, and any live leaderboard value.
- The full list of 50 paintings, and any variant beyond the three examples Steam named.
- Whether the 13 November build keeps the demo’s 18 October 2026 feature set. The demo was listed as available on the day of the check. The full game was not.
